HOW IT WORKS

A frictionless 4-step automated workflow.

By orchestrating optical cameras, RFID tags, boom barriers, and loadcell indicators into an autonomous pipeline, NIYA INFO transforms traditional weighbridges into high-throughput digital assets.

STEP 01

Vehicle Arrival & ID

Optical ANPR cameras automatically detect the license plate while Long-Range RFID readers verify truck authenticity at the entry barrier.

STEP 02

Positioning & Sensors

Infrared positioning sensors ensure the vehicle is properly aligned on the weigh platform before locking the weight to prevent axle cheat tactics.

STEP 03

Zero-Tamper Weighing

Gross and Tare weights are digitally captured directly from the weighing indicator and sealed with a cryptographic timestamp to prevent manual tampering.

STEP 04

Cloud Sync & Slip

Data instantly syncs to your central ERP / SAP / Tally system while automated digital slips are sent via SMS & WhatsApp to transport managers.
